The Forecast dashboard shows how much revenue your team is likely to close in a quarter. It also shows how that number is changing and which opportunities make it up. Amolino builds this forecast for you from the activity on each deal, so you don't have to collect a commit call from every rep.

## Opening the Forecast dashboard

1. Sign in to Amolino.
2. In the left sidebar, under **Dashboard**, click **Workspace**.
3. Click the **Forecast** tab at the top of the page.

The **Manager** and **Sales rep** tabs next to it show other views of your pipeline. This guide covers only the **Forecast** tab.

<img src="/images/help/dashboard/forecast/overview.jpg" alt="The Forecast dashboard showing the Amolino forecast chart, forecast updates and the list of opportunities" width="1600" height="1000" />

## What's on the page

The dashboard has three main areas:

| Area | What it shows |
| ---- | ------------- |
| **Amolino forecast** (top left) | Your forecast total for the quarter, how much it moved in the last 7 days, the best and worst case, and a chart that breaks the number down by forecast category. See [Understanding the forecast chart](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/understanding-the-forecast-chart). |
| **Forecast updates** (top right) | The latest changes to opportunities in the forecast, such as a new value or a new close date, and how each one moved the forecast. See [Forecast updates](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/forecast-updates). |
| **Opportunities** (bottom) | Every opportunity in this forecast, which you can search, sort and filter. See [Opportunities and filters](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/opportunities-and-filters). |

## Choosing a quarter and pipeline

Use the controls in the top-right corner to choose what the dashboard shows.

<img src="/images/help/dashboard/forecast/header-controls.jpg" alt="Quarter selector, pipeline selector and Forecast targets button" width="1600" height="72" />

### Quarter

The dashboard opens on the current quarter. To see a different one:

- Click the **‹** or **›** arrows to step back or forward one quarter, or
- Click the quarter name (for example **Q4 - 2026**). Use the arrows next to the year to change the year, then pick a quarter.

<img src="/images/help/dashboard/forecast/quarter-picker.jpg" alt="Quarter picker showing Q1 through Q4 for 2026" width="1080" height="530" />

Each quarter lists its date range. For example, **Q4: Oct 1 - Jan 1** covers October 1 through December 31. An opportunity counts toward a quarter when its close date falls inside that range.

<Note>
The quarter and year you pick are saved in the page address. You can bookmark the page or share the link with a teammate, and they'll see the same quarter.
</Note>

### Pipeline

If your CRM has more than one pipeline, use the **All pipelines** menu to look at just one of them. The forecast, chart, updates and opportunity list all change to match. Choose **All** to go back to the combined view.

### Forecast targets

Click **Forecast targets** to see or set the revenue target for each pipeline and quarter. The target appears as a line across the forecast chart. See [Setting forecast targets](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/forecast-targets).

## Amolino forecast vs. CRM forecast

The **Amolino / CRM** switch above the chart lets you change which forecast you're looking at:

- **Amolino** (the default) is the forecast Amolino builds by looking at what is actually happening on each deal.
- **CRM** is based on the forecast categories your reps set in your CRM. Its chart uses your CRM's categories: **Pipeline**, **Not forecasted**, **Commit**, **Closed won**, **Best case** and **Forecast**.

Comparing the two shows where your reps' view of a deal differs from the evidence Amolino sees.

<Note>
If the CRM forecast shows **$0**, Amolino didn't find a forecast category on your opportunities in the CRM. Check that your reps fill in the forecast category field and that your CRM is connected under **Connect accounts**.
</Note>
