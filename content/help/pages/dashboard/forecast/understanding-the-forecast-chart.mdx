The **Amolino forecast** panel at the top left of the Forecast dashboard shows how much revenue you're likely to close this quarter and what that number is made of.

<ForecastWidget />

## The forecast number

The large number (for example **<DemoValue path="forecast.total" />**) is your **Amolino forecast** for the selected quarter and pipeline.

The figure next to it (for example **<DemoValue path="forecast.change7d" /> last 7 days**) shows how much the forecast has moved over the past week. Red means it went down; green means it went up. To see which deals caused the change, open [Forecast updates](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/forecast-updates).

## Forecast categories

Amolino puts every opportunity into a **forecast category**. The category is based on what is happening on the deal, such as recent emails and meetings, how engaged the buyer is, and whether the close date keeps moving. It doesn't rely on the stage a rep entered by hand.

| Category | What it means |
| -------- | ------------- |
| **Closed won** | The deal has already been won. |
| **Confident** | Amolino expects this deal to close this quarter. |
| **Upside** | The deal could close this quarter, but there are signs it may slip. |
| **Accelerator** | The deal is currently off track for this quarter, but it could still be won with extra effort. These are the deals to push on if you need to close a gap. |
| **Unlikely** | Amolino doesn't expect this deal to close this quarter. |
| **New** | The opportunity is too new for Amolino to assess yet. |
| **Lost** | The deal has been lost. |

You can see each opportunity's category in the **Amolino forecast** column of the opportunity list.

## Worst case, forecast and best case

Amolino adds categories together to give you three numbers:

| Number | Includes |
| ------ | -------- |
| **Worst case** | Closed won + Confident |
| **Forecast** | Closed won + Confident + Upside |
| **Best case** | Closed won + Confident + Upside + Accelerator |

**Unlikely**, **New** and **Lost** opportunities are not counted in any of the three.

The **Best case** and **Worst case** totals are shown in the bar above the chart. Click either one to filter the opportunity list to just the deals that make up that number. See [Opportunities and filters](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/opportunities-and-filters).

## Reading the chart

The chart is a waterfall. Reading from left to right, it builds the forecast one category at a time:

1. **Closed won**: revenue already won.
2. **Confident**: stacks on top of Closed won.
3. **Worst case**: the running total so far (Closed won + Confident).
4. **Upside**: stacks on top of the worst case.
5. **Forecast**: the running total (worst case + Upside).
6. **Accelerator**: stacks on top of the forecast.
7. **Best case**: the final total (forecast + Accelerator).

Solid bars (Closed won, Confident, Upside, Accelerator) are the amount each category adds. Striped bars (Worst case, Forecast, Best case) are running totals.

The dark horizontal line marks your **target** for the quarter, for example **Target: <DemoValue path="forecast.target" />**. The distance between the bars and the line shows how far you are from target. To change the target, see [Setting forecast targets](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/forecast-targets).

### Bar details

Hover over any bar to see its details:

- The **value** is the amount that category adds (for example, Accelerator adds **<DemoValue path="bars.accelerator.value" />**).
- **Base** is where the bar starts: the running total before this category.
- **Peak** is where the bar ends: the running total after this category.

In the example above, Accelerator deals would take the forecast from **<DemoValue path="bars.accelerator.base" />** (base) to a best case of **<DemoValue path="bars.accelerator.peak" />** (peak).
