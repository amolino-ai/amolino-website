The bottom half of the Forecast dashboard lists every opportunity in the selected quarter and pipeline. Use it to see exactly which deals make up your forecast and to find the ones that need attention.

## Showing and hiding the list

The number above the list (for example **525**) is how many opportunities are included in this forecast. Click **Hide opportunities** to collapse the list, and **Show opportunities** to bring it back.

## Columns

| Column | What it shows |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **Opportunity name** | The deal. Click the name to open it in a new tab. |
| **Customer** | The company the deal is with. Click it to open the company. |
| **Amolino forecast** | The deal's forecast category, such as **Confident**, **Upside**, **Accelerator** or **Unlikely**. See [Forecast categories](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/understanding-the-forecast-chart#forecast-categories). |
| **Value** | The deal amount. |
| **Stage** | The deal's current stage in your CRM pipeline. |
| **Close date** | The expected close date. |
| **Health** | Amolino's assessment of the deal's health (see below). |

### Deal health

Amolino scores how healthy each deal is, based on things like engagement, activity and blockers. The score falls into one of four levels:

| Health | What it means |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **Healthy** | The deal is on track. |
| **Progressing** | The deal is moving forward but needs some attention. |
| **At risk** | There are warning signs. Review the deal soon. |
| **Critical** | The deal is in serious trouble and needs action now. |

If there isn't enough information to score a deal yet, its health is **Unknown**.

## Searching and sorting

- **Search**: type in **Search opportunity** to find a deal by name.
- **Sort**: click a column header (**Opportunity name**, **Value**, **Stage**, **Close date** or **Health**) to sort by it, or choose from the sort menu on the right. Click the same header again to reverse the order.
- **Page size**: use the **Displaying** menu at the bottom of the list to show 7, 14 or 21 opportunities per page, and the page buttons to move between pages.

## Filtering from the forecast

The quickest way to filter is from the forecast panel. Click **Best case** or **Worst case** above the chart, and the list narrows to just the opportunities in that total. For example, clicking **Best case** filters to Closed won, Confident, Upside and Accelerator deals.

The active filters appear as chips next to **Showing N results for:**. Click the **×** on a chip to remove that filter.

## Using the filter panel

Click **Show filters** to open the filter panel on the right. The number next to **Hide filters** tells you how many filters are active.

Click a filter name to expand it. You can combine as many filters as you need.

### Deal details

| Filter | Options |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Customer** | Pick one or more companies. |
| **Owner** | Pick one or more deal owners. |
| **Products** | Pick one or more products on the deal. |
| **Value** | Between two amounts, equal to an amount, empty or not empty. |
| **Created date** | A date range for when the opportunity was created. |
| **Pipeline** | One or more pipelines. |
| **Pipeline stage** | Stages within each pipeline. Use **Reset** to clear the stages for a pipeline. |
| **Channel partners** | Deals that involve a particular vendor, distributor or reseller. |

### Forecast

| Filter | Options |
| ------ | ------- |
| **CRM forecast** | The forecast category set in your CRM: Pipeline, Not forecasted, Most likely, Commit, Closed won or Best case. |
| **Amolino forecast** | Amolino's forecast category: New, Confident, Upside, Accelerator, Unlikely, Closed won or Lost. |
| **Health** | Healthy, Progressing, At risk or Critical. |

Filtering by **CRM forecast** and **Amolino forecast** together is a quick way to find deals where your rep and Amolino disagree. For example, deals a rep marked **Commit** that Amolino rates **Unlikely**.

### Risk signals

| Filter | Options |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Blockers** | Deals with any blockers, at least 2, at least 3, or a custom range. |
| **Pushed** | How many times the close date has been pushed out. Set a minimum and maximum. |
| **Activity** | By activity type (email exchanges, meetings held, Microsoft Teams, Slack messages, files shared) and by how recent it is (last 7, 14 or 30 days, or **No activity**). |
| **Qualifications** | Deals that have or are missing qualification criteria: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identify pain, Champion and Competition. |
| **Competitors** | Deals that involve specific competitors, or a minimum number of competitors. |

### Clearing filters

Click **Clear filters** at the bottom of the filter panel to remove all filters at once. Click **Hide filters** to close the panel.

## Useful filter combinations

- **Deals that can close the gap**: Amolino forecast = **Accelerator**, sorted by **Value**.
- **Committed deals at risk**: CRM forecast = **Commit** and Health = **At risk** or **Critical**.
- **Stalled deals**: Activity = **No activity** in the last 14 days.
- **Slipping deals**: Pushed with a minimum of 2.
- **Gaps in qualification**: Qualifications missing **Economic buyer** or **Champion**.
