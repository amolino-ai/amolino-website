Forecast targets are the revenue goals for each pipeline in each quarter. Once you set a target, it appears as a line across the [forecast chart](/help/pages/dashboard/forecast/understanding-the-forecast-chart), so you can see at a glance how close your forecast is to the goal.

## Viewing targets

1. Open the **Forecast** tab of your dashboard.
2. Click **Forecast targets** in the top-right corner.

A panel opens with the targets for the current fiscal year.

<img src="/images/help/dashboard/forecast/forecast-targets.jpg" alt="Forecast targets panel showing quarterly targets for each pipeline" width="1600" height="835" />

| Column | What it shows |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **Pipeline** | Each pipeline in your CRM. |
| **Q1 – Q4** | The target for each quarter. A blank cell means no target has been set. |
| **Annual** | The total of the four quarterly targets. |
| **Attainment** | How much of the annual target has been closed so far, shown as a percentage. A dash (-) means there is no target to measure against. |

The **Total** row adds up every pipeline.

To look at another year, use the **FY** menu in the top-left corner of the panel.

## Editing targets

1. In the Forecast targets panel, click **Edit targets**.
2. Enter a target in each quarter you want to set. Leave a cell blank if a pipeline has no target for that quarter.
3. Save your changes.

The chart updates to show the new target line.

<Note>
Depending on your role, you may not be able to edit targets. If **Edit targets** isn't available, ask an Amolino admin in your organization.
</Note>

## How targets appear on the chart

When **All pipelines** is selected, the chart shows the total target for the quarter. When you pick a single pipeline, it shows that pipeline's target.
