# HubSpot Integration

This guide will walk you through setting up the HubSpot integration with Amolino.

## Prerequisites

You need to be an admin in your HubSpot account and in Amolino.

## Step 1: Go to the Apps & Integrations Page

1. Sign in to your Amolino account.
2. Select your organization and click on the gear icon next to it.
3. Go to the "[Apps & integrations](https://app.amolino.ai/organization_settings?activeTab=appsIntegrations)" tab.

![Apps & Integrations screen](/images/help/integrations/appsnintegrations.png)

## Step 2: Connect HubSpot

Find HubSpot in the list of available integrations towards the bottom of the page (under "Apps").
Click on "Connect" to start the integration process.

<img src="/images/help/integrations/hubspot/hubspot.png" alt="HubSpot button" width="301"/>

A popup will appear asking you to authorize the connection. Follow the
instructions. The popup will close automatically at the end of the process, and
HubSpot will be connected.
