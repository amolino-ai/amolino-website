# Google Workspace Integration

This guide will walk you through setting up the Google Workspace integration with Amolino.

## Prerequisites

You need to be an admin in your Google Workspace and in Amolino.

## Step 1: Add AmolinoAI as a Trusted App

1. Sign in to your Google Admin console at [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com).
2. From the Admin console Home page, go to **Security** > **Access and data control** > **API controls** > [**Manage Third-Party App Access**](https://admin.google.com/u/3/ac/owl/list?tab=configuredApps).
3. Add Amolino as trusted.

## Step 2: Allow Domain-Wide Delegation

![Apps & Integrations screen](/images/help/integrations/google-workspace/domainwidedelegation.webp)

1. From the Admin console Home page, go to **Security** > **Access and data control** > **API controls** > [**Manage Domain Wide Delegation**](https://admin.google.com/u/3/ac/owl/domainwidedelegation).
2. Click on **Add new** and enter the following details.

**Client ID:**

> 110255286194196618640

**Scopes:**

> openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.readonly, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.domain.readonly


## Step 3: Go to the Apps & Integrations Page in Amolino

1. Sign in to your Amolino account.
2. Select your organization and click on the gear icon next to it.
3. Go to the "[Apps & integrations](https://app.amolino.ai/organization_settings?activeTab=appsIntegrations)" tab.

![Apps & Integrations screen](/images/help/integrations/appsnintegrations.png)

## Step 4: Connect Google Workspace

Find Google Workspace in the list of available integrations towards the bottom of the page (under "Apps").
Click on "Connect" to start the integration process.

<img src="/images/help/integrations/google-workspace/googlebtn.png" alt="Google Workspace button" width="301"/>

A popup will appear, showing you the same instructions you just completed. Then,
it will ask you to authorize the connection. Follow the instructions. The popup
will close automatically at the end of the process, and Google Workspace will be
connected.
